# Technology icon attribution

These technology icons are sourced from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), revision [`6a37858047b0e8d9c9c0e68f4a071fc410652e89`](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/6a37858047b0e8d9c9c0e68f4a071fc410652e89).

Each local `<technology>.svg` uses the upstream file `icons/<technology>/<technology>-original.svg`. The included technologies are `vuejs`, `typescript`, `react`, `javascript`, `html5`, `css3`, `tailwindcss`, `vuetify`, `nodejs`, `express`, `mongodb`, `redis`, `docker`, `git`, `github`, `gitlab`, `vitest`, and `playwright`.

The Express and GitHub icons include an added white circular background with a gray border to retain contrast in light and dark themes. Their original artwork is preserved inside the circle.

Product names and logos identify their respective technologies and belong to their respective owners. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement.
